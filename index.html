About

Built at the bedside, for the people learning at it.

Most of hepatology is pattern recognition, and patterns are easier to learn when you can move them. That is why these tools are interactive.

I’m a transplant hepatologist and gastroenterologist at Tufts Medical Center in Boston. I trained in internal medicine at St. Elizabeth’s Medical Center, gastroenterology at SUNY Downstate, and transplant hepatology at Lahey Clinic. These tools came out of my clinic, my teaching and my research, and out of wanting complex liver ideas to be easier to use.
